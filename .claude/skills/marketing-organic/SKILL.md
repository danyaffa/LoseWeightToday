# Free Media and Discovery

Read [shared policy](references/core-policy.md) first. Use [sources](references/sources.md) for current-source checks and boundaries.

1. Audit owned content and actual audience questions, search results and relevant communities. Reuse one verified source asset into a platform-native short video, carousel, practical article, FAQ, newsletter draft and founder post. Adapt the message and CTA; do not spam identical posts everywhere.
2. Prioritize useful evergreen answers, comparison/use-case pages, clear product facts, internal links, descriptive titles, accessible text and images, canonical URLs, valid sitemap/robots behavior and appropriate factual structured data. Check actual crawlability and indexing signals. Do not promise Google or AI-agent inclusion; llms.txt is not a universal ranking switch.
   AI-search visibility: keep a fixed list of 10–20 real buyer questions. Each week, ask them in the AI assistants actually available (for example ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) and record whether the brand is mentioned or linked, which rivals are, and the sources cited. Close the gaps with factual pages that answer those questions. Run a weekly technical check of the site: broken links, titles, indexing, sitemap and page speed.
3. For eligible local businesses review their existing Google Business Profile, real services, hours, contact details, location consistency and permitted review practices. Do not create fake locations or incentivized/fabricated reviews. Health review/testimonial restrictions require a separate check.
4. Suggest relevant partnerships, interviews, podcasts, expert contributions, useful community answers and genuine customer education. Check community promotion rules and audience fit. Draft outreach only; sending requires explicit recipient/campaign authority and appropriate consent.
5. Create a four-week calendar with objective, audience, format, source asset, channel, owner, CTA, publish time and tracking. Surface high-effort channels and tool costs. 'No media spend' does not mean unlimited free data, free APIs or free production.
6. Track referral visits, engaged visitors, qualified leads, booked calls and sales where available. Compare quality and contribution, not vanity engagement alone. Feed successful organic messages into paid tests only within authorized scope.

Return the five best opportunities ranked by evidence, effort and route to conversion, including one practical action possible without adding a paid service.
