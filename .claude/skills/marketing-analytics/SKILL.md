# Analytics and Continuous Learning

Read [shared policy](references/core-policy.md) first. Use [sources](references/sources.md) for current-source checks and boundaries.

1. Read authorized first-party ad, website, CRM and transaction records. Align currency, date ranges, attribution windows, timezone and definitions. Label platform-attributed revenue separately from reconciled transaction revenue; do not sum overlapping attribution claims across networks.
2. Compute CTR=clicks/impressions, CPC=spend/clicks, CPL=spend/valid leads, qualified CPL=spend/qualified leads, CPA=spend/attributed acquisitions, ROAS=attributed revenue/spend. Zero denominators produce undefined/null, not infinity or success. Explain ROAS is revenue, not profit; include margin, refunds and relevant production/service costs where verified.
3. Reconcile conversion event IDs/order IDs, refunds, cancellations, test traffic, duplicates and late-arriving events. Keep attribution hypotheses explicit. Never backfill unknown competitor metrics with own-account averages.
4. Compare creative cohorts under similar audience, spend, placement and time conditions. Assess sufficient evidence and uncertainty rather than declaring a winner after one purchase. Predefine the experiment and primary metric; treat repeated peeking and multiple tests as sources of false discoveries.
5. Detect potential fatigue from repeated comparable observations: falling engagement/conversion with rising frequency or cost. Treat changes in audience, auction or landing page as alternative explanations. Propose refreshes and a control experiment; do not auto-pause/scale without the required authority.
   Grade every live own ad against the account's comparable median (A above, B near, C below, D far below on the primary metric, with enough spend to judge) and give each C or D ad one specific fix: hook, offer, visual, audience or landing page.
6. Report learning in a reusable ledger: hypothesis, creative version, scope, data window, spend, result, uncertainty, decision and next test. Persist only verified lessons. Negative experiments and unknowns are useful evidence.

Produce a plain daily/weekly report centered on qualified leads, bookings, sales and contribution, with spend and blockers visible. Never promise top market results or guaranteed sales.
