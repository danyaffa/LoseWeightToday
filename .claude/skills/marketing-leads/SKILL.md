# Prospects and Lead Qualification

Read [shared policy](references/core-policy.md) first. Use [sources](references/sources.md) for current-source checks and boundaries.

1. Define the ideal customer and explicit exclusions: industry, location, size, role, need, timing and budget. Identify relevant public business directories, search results, inbound submissions and authorized enrichment services. Paid contact datasets require access and budget; a skill does not contain Apollo's database.
2. Save source URL, business identity, retrieval time, fit rationale and permitted contact context. Do not infer sensitive traits or scrape private profiles. Public contact information does not establish consent to commercial messaging.
3. Deduplicate by normalized verified business domain plus provider IDs/contact identity, preserving source provenance. Keep brands separate. Mark uncertain identity matches for review instead of merging different people.
4. Score fit, expressed intent and known need separately, then prioritize explainably. Missing budget or role is unknown, not a zero-confidence rejection. Never claim a person intends to buy solely because of demographic resemblance or an AI guess.
5. For inbound forms verify server validation, anti-abuse/rate limiting, tenant assignment, consent and delivery. Route to the appropriate existing owner and log state transitions. Exclude test leads from commercial reports.
6. Enrich only through authorized sources and record allowed use. Maintain suppression, opt-out and retention controls. Prepare a personalized first-contact draft referencing an actual business issue, without invented familiarity or private facts.

Return qualified prospects with evidence and a recommended next action. Do not send, purchase lists, invite, call or bulk message unless separately authorized for the defined recipients/campaign.
